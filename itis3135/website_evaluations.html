CRAP Website Evaluations

I will be comparing two websites, badhtml and kawaiiattic against CRAP principles.

badhtml vs kawaiiattic

  • Contrast:badhtml violates the contrast principle with everything being the same font, weird snowflakes blocking some words, and highly saturated backgrounuds that make the words even harder to see.
    Meanwhile, kawaiiattic uses a mix of soft pink and white values, representing this principle aesthetically and in a clean manner.
  • Repition:While badhtml does generally maintain the same font throughout, it uses an unruly clash of button styles and color palettes, resulting in the website being hard to look at.
    When it comes to kawaiiattic though, it is a lot more harmonic visually through its consistent soft fonts, button styles, and appropiate heading formats.
  • Alignment:badhtml is not consistent in structure due to having an unclear grid structure, texts overflowing from borders, and lack of professionalism.
    In contrast, kawaiiattic is evenly centered with clear grids dividing each section.
  • Proximity:Although badhtml possesses some attempt at unity through its sectioned structure, it lacks titles and context in a few areas.
    Switching back to kawaiiattic, everything is appropiately grouped accordingly and easy to navigate.
