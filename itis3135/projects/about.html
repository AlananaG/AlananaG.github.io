About Us

Luxury Browns Sweets & Treats is a small black-owned bakery shop run by Sharon Walker and located in Greensboro, North Carolina. Founded in 2021, our cakers and bakers serve handcrafted cakes, pastries, cookies, and more to provide you the luxury you deserve. We look forwarded to bringing splendid joy to your day and one more thing for your family to bond over.
