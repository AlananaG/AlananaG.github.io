Project Overview

Application Description & Purpose

This website is dedicated to Sharon Walker's small business, Luxury Browns Sweets & Treats, which specializes in homemade baked goods such as cakes, cobblers, cookies, and cupcakes.

The purpose of this website is to promote the business, showcase products, inform customers about upcoming events, and provide a way for users to contact the business for inquiries. The website serves as a digital storefront to improve visibility and customer engagement.

Intended Audience

  • Customers interested in purchasing baked goods
  • Event attendees
  • Community members supporting local businesses

Overview of Website Content

  • Business introduction
  • Product menu
  • Image gallery
  • Events schedule
  • Contact information and inquiry form
