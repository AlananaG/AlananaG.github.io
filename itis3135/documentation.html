Gecko Documentation

Gecko Introduction

Geckos are small lizards known for their climbing ability, wide eyes, and unique toe pads.

Many geckos are active at night and live in warm habitats.

gecko = "climber"
  • Small reptiles
  • Strong climbers

Gecko Habitats

Different geckos live in deserts, rainforests, rocky areas, and homes.

Their habitat affects how they hide, hunt, and survive.

habitat = "rainforest"
  • Desert
  • Rainforest
  • Tropical areas

Gecko Diet

Most geckos eat insects and small bugs.

Some geckos also enjoy fruit based foods depending on the species.

food = "crickets"
  • Crickets
  • Mealworms

Gecko Behavior

Geckos may hide during the day and become active at night.

Some geckos make sounds, drop their tails, or change color slightly.

behavior = "nocturnal"
  • Hiding
  • Climbing

Gecko Care

Pet geckos need a clean enclosure, safe heating, proper food, and hiding spots.

Good care helps geckos stay healthy and less stressed.

care = "clean habitat"
  • Heat source
  • Fresh water
