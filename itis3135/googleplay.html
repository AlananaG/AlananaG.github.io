Google Play Interactive Dashboard | December 2025

My Entry for HAVK's Data Analysis Hackathon

In this competition, I chose to analyze user behavior through a google play dataset on Kaggle with a partner. This topic was chosen in order to help developers and users alike to better understand what is typically in demand and is most popular. The dashboard features curated filters such as whether or not an app is free and what category one would like to explore. Warm colors are primarily used to portray a fun, attractive, and entertaining atmosphere.
