All were very fun experiences as I got the chance to learn more about how it's like to both collaborate and coordinate tasks effectively in a more time sensitive setting. Additionally, I joined the HAVK organization, a coding club at UNC Charlotte that specialized in small competitions and resume building. Moments like these where I shared my work with others is what truly sparked my interest in the field.
